MARKET RISK ANALYTICS

Risk Detection & Assessment

The system evaluates unusual price movements and trading-volume behaviour to identify market activity that may require further investigation.

Risk Classification

Each detected market event is assigned a risk level according to the calculated risk score.

60–100

High Risk

Indicates a combination of stronger price movement and abnormal trading volume. These observations require closer review.

30–59

Medium Risk

Indicates noticeable market activity where one or more indicators show unusual behaviour.

0–29

Low Risk

Indicates relatively lower abnormality based on the price and volume indicators used by the system.

Risk Assessment Methodology

The system calculates a risk score by combining price movement and trading-volume indicators. The resulting score is used to classify the observation into High, Medium or Low risk.

Price Movement

The system calculates the percentage change between consecutive market observations. Larger movements contribute more to the overall risk score.

Volume Ratio

Current trading volume is compared with the previous rolling volume baseline. A larger volume ratio indicates unusual trading activity.

Combined Risk Score

Price and volume contributions are combined to produce a score between 0 and 100.

Suspicious Event

An observation is highlighted when unusual price movement occurs together with abnormal trading volume.

Risk Score Interpretation

Reference ranges used by the detection system.

Risk Level Score Range Interpretation
High 60 – 100 Strong abnormal market indicators
Medium 30 – 59 Noticeable unusual market activity
Low 0 – 29 Lower detected abnormality

Detection Indicators

The system focuses on two major market indicators.

📈 Price Spike Detection

The system monitors percentage price changes between consecutive observations. Significant changes are treated as potential abnormal price activity.

📊 Volume Spike Detection

The system compares current trading volume with a rolling historical baseline to identify unusually high trading activity.
