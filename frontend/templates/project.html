Smart Market

Smart Market Price Manipulation Detection System is a data-driven market surveillance project designed to identify unusual price and trading-volume activity and classify detected events using automated risk scoring.

Project Overview

The Smart Market Price Manipulation Detection System is a final-year Data Science and Analytics project. The system analyses financial market data to identify unusual combinations of price movement and trading volume.


The application provides market analysis, suspicious event detection, automated risk scoring, risk-level classification and downloadable reports through a web-based interface.

Project Details

Project Name

Smart Market Price Manipulation Detection System

Project Type

Final Year Data Science & Analytics Project

Main Objective

Detect unusual price and trading-volume activity in financial market data.

Application Type

Web-based market surveillance and risk analysis application.

Project Objectives

  • Analyse financial market price and trading-volume data.
  • Identify unusual price movements.
  • Detect abnormal trading-volume activity.
  • Combine price and volume indicators to identify suspicious events.
  • Calculate an automated risk score for detected market activity.
  • Generate reports containing suspicious market events.
