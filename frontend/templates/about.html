SmartMarket

A market surveillance and risk analysis system designed to identify unusual price movements and abnormal trading-volume patterns.

About Smart Market

Smart Market Price Manipulation Detection System is a data-driven market surveillance application developed to identify unusual activity in financial market data.


The system analyses market price and trading-volume information and detects unusual combinations of price movement and trading activity. It then calculates a risk score to help classify detected events into different risk levels.

Key Features

📊

Market Analysis

Analyse current intraday market data using price and trading-volume information.

⚠️

Risk Detection

Identify unusual market activity and calculate a risk score for detected events.

📄

Report Generation

Generate suspicious-event reports and download the results as CSV files.

How The System Works

1

Market Data

Current market information is retrieved for the selected stock.

2

Detection

Price and volume patterns are analysed for unusual activity.

3

Risk Scoring

Detected activity receives a numerical risk score and risk level.

4

Report

Suspicious events can be reviewed and downloaded as a report.

Project Technology

The system is developed using Python and Flask for the backend, Pandas for data processing, yfinance for intraday market-data retrieval, and HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the frontend.
